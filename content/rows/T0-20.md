what_failed: "The endpoint in your extensions.operations version 3 entry is not HTTPS, or comes without web.browser_session_endpoint. Assistants ignore an operations entry at any other version, so without a version 3 entry the check does not apply. A browser-control entry without web.browser_session_endpoint gets a note."
why_it_matters: "Each version of the protocol defines one version of the operations extension, and assistants ignore an entry at any other version. Version 1.0 defines version 3, which can add an endpoint for approvals on your website. Without one, operations and approvals travel through the same API, tool or conversation as the action."
